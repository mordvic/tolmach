In the context of the ongoing effort to improve our internal processes, it should be noted that the current document approval workflow, which is at the present time being carried out by the members of our department on a manual basis, takes an unreasonably long amount of time and requires the involvement of a significant number of specialists, which in turn has resulted in the failure to meet deadlines across a whole range of areas. In order to ensure the fulfilment of the plan, we kindly ask that a review of the aforementioned procedure be carried out as soon as possible.

As of today, there exists a situation in which every document that is subject to approval passes through five separate stages of review, despite the fact that the actual necessity of such a number of stages has never been confirmed, justified, or recorded in any of the currently effective regulations approved by the management of the organisation. This situation requires immediate attention. This procedure needs to be simplified.

In view of the above, we propose to carry out a reduction of the number of approval stages to two, and we also propose to carry out a migration of all correspondence on this matter to electronic form as soon as possible.
