Colleagues,

From 6 October the procedure for approving business trips changes. A request must be submitted through the portal no later than 10 working days before departure. Requests submitted later will not be considered; the exception is an urgent trip backed by a letter from the head of the division.

The accommodation limit is 7,500 roubles a night, and the daily allowance is 1,200 roubles. Expenses above the limit are reimbursed only with a memo approved before the trip, not after it.

The trip report is handed to accounting within 3 working days of your return. Original receipts are attached to the report; photographs of receipts are not accepted.

If you have questions, contact Marina Orlova, extension 2114.
