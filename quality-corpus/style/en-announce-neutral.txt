The library on Sadovaya Street closes for renovation from 3 November. The work will last until the end of January; we will announce the exact reopening date separately.

During the renovation, books can be returned and collected at the branch at 24 Mira Avenue. The branch is open Tuesday to Saturday from 10:00 to 19:00. The return date for all books borrowed before the closure is automatically extended to 15 February, and no fines will be charged for this period.

The reading room and the children's section will not operate during the renovation. The reading club for schoolchildren moves to the Zarya community centre; the timetable stays the same — Wednesdays at 16:30.

After the renovation the library will have a new children's room, an area for working with laptops, and a ramp at the main entrance.
